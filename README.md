# Build a Professional E-commerce Website – NABSTORE

Create a modern, professional, minimalist, and premium-looking e-commerce website named **NABSTORE**.

## 1. Brand & Identity
- Brand name: NABSTORE
- Create an original, modern, minimalist logo. Avoid generic paw-print logos.
- Choose a professional color palette suitable for an international technology and pet-products brand.
- Brand positioning: High-quality products at competitive prices.
- Design must look polished, trustworthy, and commercially professional.

## 2. Market & Language
- Target market: Iran.
- Default language: Persian.
- Add a Persian / English language switch.
- Fully support Persian RTL and English LTR layouts.
- Display prices in Iranian Toman.
- Use mobile-first, responsive design.

## 3. Products & Categories
The main focus is pet products, especially cats and dogs, alongside trending gadgets.

Create these categories:
- Pet Beds & Comfort
- Smart Food & Water Bowls
- Hygiene & Cleaning
- Travel & Carriers
- Pet Care Products
- Smart Pet Gadgets
- Pet Accessories & Clothing
- Pet Toys & Entertainment
- Trending Tech Gadgets
- Innovative Products

Add a special homepage section named **Trending Now**.

## 4. Homepage Design
Create an attractive homepage including:
- Professional hero section with promotional banners.
- Featured product categories.
- Trending products.
- New arrivals.
- Best sellers.
- Special offers and discounts.
- Recommended products.
- Customer reviews.
- Professional footer with contact and social links.

Keep the design clean, modern, and easy to navigate.

## 5. Essential E-commerce Features
Implement:
- Product search and advanced filters.
- Product detail pages with images, descriptions, prices, and stock status.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Customer registration and login using mobile number.
- User dashboard and order history.
- Wishlist and product comparison.
- Discount codes and promotions.
- Product ratings and reviews.
- Related product recommendations.
- Order status tracking.
- Customer support / contact section.
- SEO-friendly blog.

## 6. Admin Dashboard
Create a secure admin panel to manage:
- Products, categories, images, and descriptions.
- Prices and inventory.
- Customer accounts.
- Orders and order statuses.
- Discounts and promotions.
- Blog posts and homepage banners.

The inventory system must support two supply methods:
1. Products stored in our own warehouse.
2. Products supplied directly by external suppliers.

## 7. Technical Requirements
- Use a reliable full-stack architecture.
- Prefer React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS for the frontend.
- Use Supabase or another suitable database and backend.
- Implement secure authentication and database access.
- Keep the project connected to GitHub if supported.
- Prepare the architecture for future payment gateway, SMS, and shipping integrations in Iran.
- Payment and SMS integrations must be real and configurable, not simulated.
- Never display fake stock, prices, reviews, or payment confirmations.

## 8. Important Development Instructions
Build the project in stages to avoid unnecessary complexity.

**First stage:** Create the complete visual design, homepage, navigation, product categories, sample product cards, and responsive Persian RTL layout.

**Second stage:** Implement product pages, search, filters, shopping cart, and customer accounts.

**Third stage:** Implement database, admin dashboard, inventory, orders, and integrations.

Prioritize excellent design, usability, performance, security, and scalability.

The final result should look like a real international online store, not a basic template or demo website.
